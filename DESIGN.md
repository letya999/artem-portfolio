# Visual direction

The owner rejected all three static mockups and requested a complex 3D hero inspired by the rotating Kafka head in Prague, combined with a big-bang assembly. Two owner-supplied photographs now replace the earlier absence of portrait assets. The follow-up authorizes a working browser prototype.

Light monochrome editorial layout, oversized Oswald name and Manrope text, restrained green accents. The central object is a volumetric portrait built from 64 closed contour plates. Facial geometry uses 478 inferred landmarks from the owner's portrait; the unseen cranium is approximated. Plates perform staggered complete rotations in a 12-second repeating cycle, with aligned intervals for recognition. Rounded chrome backs and polished caps replace the former rectangular slabs. Head rotation follows the viewport cursor, with an additional iris-sampling gaze shader inside the eyelids. A generated monochrome cutout preserves the supplied portrait; the second photograph appears in the biography.

The previous image comps are rejected references, not implementation specifications. Content comes from the CV; estimates and projections remain labelled. Respect native scrolling, pause controls, reduced motion and portrait fallback.
