# Visual direction

The owner rejected all three static mockups and requested a complex 3D hero inspired by the rotating Kafka head in Prague, combined with a big-bang assembly. Two owner-supplied photographs now replace the earlier absence of portrait assets. The follow-up authorizes a working browser prototype.

Light monochrome editorial layout, oversized Oswald name and Manrope text, restrained green accents. The central object is a volumetric portrait built from 42 closed contour plates. Facial geometry uses 468 pose-aligned landmarks combined from three owner-supplied photographs; the unseen cranium is approximated. Plates perform staggered complete rotations in a 18-second repeating cycle, with aligned intervals for recognition. Satin metal backs and darker caps replace the former rectangular slabs. Head rotation follows the viewport cursor, with procedural dark eye slots and moving pupils. The face uses uniform satin steel with no photograph mapped onto it; the forehead has broad planes, the nose and mouth retain measured facial depths, and the jaw follows facial landmarks. Reflections are subdued so the form stays readable. A generated monochrome cutout is retained as a loading/error fallback; the second photograph appears in the biography.

The previous image comps are rejected references, not implementation specifications. Content comes from the CV; estimates and projections remain labelled. Respect native scrolling, pause controls, reduced motion and portrait fallback.

The latest face uses a neutral closed mouth. The frontal view drives proportions, secondary views inform depth, and the hand-occluded photograph is excluded from fitting. The scalp, ears and unseen back remain sculpted approximations.
