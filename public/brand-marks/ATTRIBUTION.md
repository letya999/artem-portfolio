# Brand mark sources

The GitHub, LinkedIn and Telegram contours are adapted from [Simple Icons 11.15.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0/icons), distributed under [CC0-1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/11.15.0/LICENSE.md). Their enclosing circle or square has been removed to display the standalone silhouette requested by the owner.

The Habr and vc.ru assets are typographic representations reading “Хабр.ru” and “vc.ru”, requested by the owner, rather than official artwork. All marks identify their respective services and link to the portfolio owner's profiles. No trademark ownership is claimed.
