Portrait making-of assets

Current interactive chronology:
models/original-voxel.glb: voxel-portrait/model/portrait-detail.glb from the owner-provided voxel_portrait_threejs.zip; original project license: ../voxel-LICENSE.txt (MIT).
models/metal-first.json: public/voxel-portrait.json at local commit deca966.
models/photo-reconstruction.json: locally generated MediaPipe landmark interpolation from two owner photos (qa/clay-face.json); invisible scalp and ears are estimates, not measured scan data.
models/iphone-scan.glb: unchanged original 21.10.2026.glb from the owner-provided 21.10.2026.zip, including its photographic skin and hair texture. The captured surface contains background and clothing and is open at the rear.
models/scan-transfer.json: public/voxel-portrait.json at local commit 0102e21.
models/dense-reconstruction.json: public/voxel-portrait.json at local commit 50e4a55.
models/thumb-*.png: screenshots of the corresponding interactive models. The final model uses public/voxel-portrait.json.

Photo and measured face scan: Artem Letyushev, supplied by the site owner.
scan.json: cleaned face component from the owner-supplied scan; 32,016 measured vertices. It is an open facial surface, not a complete head.
scan-views.png: four rendered views of that same cleaned face component.
landmarks.png: visualization of extracted MediaPipe landmarks over a prepared portrait and the corresponding triangular face mesh.
MediaPipe canonical topology: https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe/blob/master/mediapipe/modules/face_geometry/data/canonical_face_model.obj (Apache-2.0).
base.png: screenshot of the original voxel portrait preview. License: ../voxel-LICENSE.txt (MIT).
combined.png and final.png: screenshots of the portfolio reconstruction. Face depth comes from the owner scan; missing rear/neck areas are estimated.
No Basel Face Model geometry or research-only fitted surfaces are distributed here.
